# Adversarial Audit of the Region Slot Reuse Proof, 2026-08-24

> **Navigation**: [Documentation Root](../README.md)

**Subject.** `COMPOSITE_REGION_REUSE_PROOF.md` at commit `de8b3f68`.
**Commissioned by the operator**, with the explicit instruction to clear context and audit
adversarially before any merge.
**Method.** Five independent agents with no access to the authoring session's conversation,
scoped by document section rather than by the author's suspicions, each mandated to refute
rather than confirm, to treat an unstated assumption as a finding even when plausible, and to
report failed attacks alongside findings so that silence is distinguishable from unreached.
Permitted materials were the document, the evidence index and its named tests, and the
obligation document at `a49555bb`. One auditor was interrupted by a session limit and resumed.

## Outcome in one paragraph

The empirical layer held. The fifth auditor spot-checked citations, figures, and test
descriptions and reported that nearly all matched the artifacts, several to an unusual standard
of care, and the only citation faults were labeling precision. The mathematical layer did not
hold as written. Four results were judged not established in their literal form, Theorem A2,
Corollary C, Lemma 4 with Theorem B1r, and Theorem B2, and the findings converged from
independent directions, three auditors on the missing address-opacity axiom, three on dangling
references to a renumbered definition, two on the composition corollaries being assertions
rather than arguments, and two on instantiation facts silently imported into the general
proofs. The audit therefore did exactly what it was commissioned to do, and the revision that
followed it is recorded in the proof document's provenance appendix.

## Verified findings, by repair family

**Family 1, missing axioms the proofs silently used.** No axiom forbade a machine instruction
from deriving a scalar from a handle's address, so a conforming machine with such an
instruction defeats every equivalence theorem through scalar output. No axiom forbade
references embedded inside aggregate values or region bytes, which defeats operand-level
confinement. No definition made a slot's content an operand of an instruction that reads it,
which a slot-reading escape instruction exploits against Lemma 4, whose final step also falsely
attributed a slot-access discipline to M3. Repair, an address-opacity axiom, a flat-values
axiom, and an operand notion covering every value an instruction reads.

**Family 2, the reference primitive.** The definition of a reference was address-extensional,
which makes Lemma 1 false across epochs under bump reuse of address intervals, while every
proof used a provenance reading. Repair, references defined by provenance.

**Family 3, hypothesis corrections.** Theorem A2 admitted a countermodel, a site inside a loop
within one arm, because its hypothesis bounded the conditional's executions and not the sites'.
Lemma 4 and Theorem B1r anchored deadness at an arbitrary enclosing loop where the argument
requires the innermost one, with a concrete counterexample through an outer-loop-dead slot.
Theorem B2 equivocated between two baselines and carried neither through, and its straight-line
case failed for sites executing more than once per scope or cycle. Theorem A1's static lift
assumed static iteration bounds that totality does not supply, an unperformed structural
induction, and binary conditionals only. Theorem B1 left slot disjointness unstated. Repairs,
per-site execution hypotheses, innermost anchoring, a same-machine baseline for B2, an explicit
static-caps hypothesis with the induction performed, and plan well-formedness.

**Family 4, composition and accounting.** Both composition sentences, in Corollary C and
Corollary B2b, asserted what requires a hybrid-chain induction. Corollary C's displayed
inequality was false against the document's own Definition 1, whose cumulative path sum also
diverges on streaming programs, and the quantity actually needed, peak per-cycle occupancy
under the plan, was never defined. Corollary B2a's copy term was not well formed over
multi-cycle paths. Repairs, per-cycle memory quantities, an occupancy-correctness statement, a
proved composition lemma, and a defined selective discipline for the hybrid.

**Family 5, instantiation standing and citations.** The fifth auditor read the verifier source
and found that break edges are joined only with each other and never compared against the loop
entry stack, so M6(b) was not discharged by the mechanism cited, and that nothing showed
`Op::Reset` placement to be verifier-enforced. It also found M6(c) contradicted as discharged
by the document's own nested-stream evidence, the obligation pin predating the corrections the
document narrates, a their-line qualifier missing from the write-accessor pin citation, an
executed label overstating one inferred clause of the M2 row, an obligation count of six above
a list of seven, and two scope statements disagreeing about which theorems carry the producer
caveat.

## Post-audit measurements by the V0.2.X line

The two verifier questions were routed to the V0.2.X session, which measured both on
2026-08-24. `Op::Reset` count is **structurally enforced**, `verify()` rejecting a removed,
duplicated, or additional mid-body `Reset` with a message naming the count, while position is
dead-code-true rather than enforced, so the auditor's M4 concern is refuted by measurement.
The break-edge reading is **correct**, the joined break state becomes the post-loop state with
no comparison to entry, and the behavior is load-bearing for dispatch, eighteen of two hundred
forty-two dispatch scopes carry arm values across their `Break` while **zero of twenty-three
genuinely iterating scopes do**, so M6(b) is an emission invariant for iterating loops, beside
streams-never-return, and a floor-style enforcement would have to distinguish the two scope
kinds, assessed by that line as a real design cost and their operator's call. Chasing the
question also exposed a misclassification in their own escape table, `Break` and `BreakIf`
transfer control with the whole operand stack and can carry a region across a scope edge, so
`NoRegion` overstates and the rows are corrected-pending on their line, with the escaping set
and totality unchanged.

## Attacks that failed, in summary

The auditors' failed attacks are evidence too. The per-instance branch bound, the cross-epoch
staleness symmetry, the copy-chain fidelity induction, the environment-reply closure, the
M6(d) back-edge treatment, the escape-window discharge against the obligation's widened
reading, and nearly every citation spot-check withstood attack. The obligation-match verdict
found nothing demanded silently missing.

## Disposition

Every verified finding is repaired in the revision recorded in the proof document, which was
renumbered cleanly, and the two items pending external input, the address-opacity instantiation
and the Break row correction commit, are marked in their rows. A fresh adversarial re-audit of
the revised document is required before any merge, because a repaired proof is exactly where
the incentive to stop looking is strongest.

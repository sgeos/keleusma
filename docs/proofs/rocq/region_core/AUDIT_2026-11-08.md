# Audit of Increment B2, 2026-11-08

> **Navigation**: [Increment B2](./README.md) | [Increment A](../core_bounds/README.md) | [Increment B1](../region_reuse/README.md)

Two fresh contexts audited increment B2 adversarially on 2026-11-08, each given the sources and
Part I of the prose proof, not the author's conclusions. One audited the semantics and the
soundness statement, and the other audited the checker, the controls and the checking script.
Neither found an unsound result, and between them they ran more than a dozen adversarial module
shapes without finding an accepted module whose reuse run diverges. Both found the model
narrower than the README implied, and the second found the pin and the controls defeatable.
Each finding below was repaired or disclosed. The repairs to the semantics, the checker, the
controls and the script were checked by execution, and the documentation repairs by reading
against the sources.

A delta check by a third fresh context then found twelve repairs closed and three with a
remaining gap. A warning silencer split by a comment still evaded the scan, half of the
`sep_only` rejection claim was unpinned, and two scripts named another increment's imports in
their trusted base. Each is corrected, the scan now removes comments first, and the README no
longer claims the pins protect every control.

## Attacks that failed

The auditors report trying the following without finding a defect.

- An observation the log could miss. Every dereference logs its outcome when it happens, and the
  host sees only the log.
- A divergence in state hidden by equal logs. The simulation relation requires the stack, the
  locals, the log, the region table and the epoch to agree, and every live handle to read the
  same bytes.
- A mismatch between the checker and the machine, including underflow, calls returning
  scalars above a live handle, and handles passed through both arms of a conditional.
- Handles left on the stack at the end of a cycle, loops of zero iterations, loops consuming a
  handle from below their entry and reallocating its site, and nested loops allocating two sites
  over a live handle. Each was accepted and agreed.
- Stale dereferences, which no accepted module can reach.
- The pin of `reuse_sound` against a direct change to its statement, which it refused.

ORDERING IS NOT A COMPLETION CRITERION. The properties below may be reached in
any order, in any number of commits, on any branch. Nothing here requires a
particular sequence, commit message, or process step.

Checked against the worktree holding branch v0.3.0.

1. GENERATED PROGRAMS ARE COMPARED
- A test generates programs rather than enumerating hand-written ones, compiles
  them, and compares execution against the reference implementation.
- Generation is seeded and deterministic, and a failing case reports enough to
  reproduce it exactly.
- The number of programs actually compared is asserted against a floor, so a run
  that generated almost nothing cannot pass as coverage.
- The programs are non-trivial: a floor on distinct shapes, or on operators
  appearing, prevents collapse to what the existing fixed matrices already cover.

2. THE GENERATOR IS HELD TO ACCOUNT
- A generated program that fails to compile or to lower fails the test rather
  than being silently discarded.
- The tree records that such a failure is the generator's defect until shown
  otherwise.

3. REACH IS SHOWN, NOT ASSUMED
- The tree records a perturbation demonstrating the comparison can fail, so "no
  divergence" is a result rather than an untested assertion.

4. THE CLAIM IS SCOPED
- The tree states what the generated coverage does not reach — at minimum that
  trapping arithmetic is excluded and why, and that a green run is not a
  correctness claim about the lowering.

5. COST IS BOUNDED
- The added runtime is stated, and does not materially lengthen the gate's
  longest phase.

6. THE SUITE
- native_codegen is clean under formatting and under lints as errors, and its
  suite passes in both float configurations.
- Any divergence found is recorded as a finding, reduced toward a minimal case,
  and described as the two implementations disagreeing rather than as a verdict
  about which is wrong.
- Any guard whose pinned figure changed has the change explained in the tree.

7. RECORDS AND TREE STATE
- The handoff for this line has state-table figures agreeing with the tree, and
  its figure guard passes.
- The working tree is clean and the branch's commits are on origin.

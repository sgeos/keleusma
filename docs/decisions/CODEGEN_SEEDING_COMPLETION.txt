order, in any number of commits, on any branch. Judge the end state of the tree,
never the sequence that produced it.

DISQUALIFYING

- A stage reported as seeded whose observable did not move, or whose run stopped
  early.
- An observable asserted without its unseeded baseline having been measured.
- A green reached by narrowing a population, dropping a subject, or relaxing an
  assertion, unless what was excluded is still reported with its cause.
- A guard that cannot fail, or whose ability to fail was never shown.
- A figure carried across a change capable of moving it.
- A suite result read from a pipeline's exit status rather than the command's.

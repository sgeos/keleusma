COMPLETION CONDITION — the coverage residual is named

Ordering is NOT a completion criterion. No particular sequence of steps, branch,
commit shape, or process is required. Judge the end state only.

Complete when ALL of the following hold.

1. THE RESIDUAL IS NAMED, NOT COUNTED
   The tree records WHICH opcodes account for the opcode instances that do not
   lower, and WHICH chunks are the ones not fully lowerable, together with the
   modules they belong to. A bare count does not satisfy this.

2. THE TWO COUNTS ARE RECONCILED OR THEIR DIVERGENCE IS STATED
   Whether the non-lowering opcode instances and the non-lowerable chunks arise
   from the same cause is stated as a measured finding, not assumed. If they do
   not line up, that is recorded explicitly.

3. EACH FIGURE CARRIES ITS POPULATION
   Every number recorded says what set it is over (how many modules, how many
   chunks). No figure appears without one.

4. MEASURED AS DATA
   The finding is derived from the compiled modules themselves rather than from
   parsing source text of the corpus or of the tooling.

5. THE INSTRUMENT'S REACH IS ESTABLISHED
   The measurement includes a check that it is capable of reporting a non-empty
   result, so an empty or trivial finding cannot be mistaken for a clean tree.

6. A RECOMMENDATION FOLLOWS FROM THE NUMBERS
   The tree records what the residual implies for the next target: a cheap gap
   worth closing, a genuine workstream, or a refusal that should stay, with the
   reason tied to the measured breakdown rather than to prior belief.

7. NO SILENT SCOPE CHANGE
   If the residual is closed as part of this work rather than only measured,
   that is stated plainly along with the resulting figures. If it is not closed,
   no document claims or implies that it was.

8. NOTHING AMBIGUOUS WAS DECIDED
   The open operator questions are untouched: the Fixed, Text, Opaque and Unit
   shared-slot representations remain unimplemented, and the region planner's
   soundness obligation is not silently discharged or restated as discharged.

9. THE SUITE IS GREEN AND THE COUNT RECONCILES
   The native_codegen suite passes with zero failures, read from cargo's own
   exit status AND from the summed per-binary counts, with the two agreeing, and
   the total is accounted for as the previous total plus exactly the tests added.

10. QUALITY GATES
    Formatting, clippy at deny-warnings over tests as well as the library, and
    the documentation build at deny-warnings are all clean.

11. NO DEAD CITATIONS
    No comment or document added or changed names a test, file, function, or
    identifier that does not exist.

If any item cannot be satisfied, say so explicitly with what was measured and
why it blocks, and satisfy every other item in full.

COMPLETION CONDITION — Order-1 subject widening

Ordering is explicitly NOT a completion criterion. Any order satisfies this.

All of the following must hold as observable properties of the tree.

1. Each seeded self-hosted stage is driven against more than one
   subject input, or the tree records why a given stage cannot be and
   names the obstacle.

2. The report states, from a run, how many subjects each seeded stage
   received, and counts separately any subject that failed to produce a
   seed. A declined subject is visible rather than silently skipped.

3. No widened run is vacuous. Where a stage's verdict cannot be moved
   for a subject, that is reported rather than counted as agreement.

4. The argument-vector count for a stream entry remains one. Subject
   variation and argument-vector variation are kept distinct in both
   the code and the report.

5. Any stage that disagrees on a newly added subject is reported as a
   finding and investigated. It is not exempted, masked, or removed.

6. A guard fails if a seeded stage is driven at only one subject, or if
   the subject set is empty. No guard pins the subject count to a
   constant.

7. The tree states the remaining limitation at its new position rather
   than implying the gate's inputs are now general.

8. The tree does not claim the Order-1 gate is met.

9. Any claim that a figure changed is supported by a recorded
   measurement of both the before and after values.

10. If the number of subjects is bounded for runtime, the bound and its
    reason are stated rather than applied silently.

11. The files recorded as owned by the other line are unmodified.

12. The native code generator's suite passes with zero failures and its
    formatting and lint checks pass, with the pass/fail judgement taken
    from something other than a truncated or piped view of the output.

13. Work that is finished is committed. Pushing may be deferred while a
    pull request of this line is mid-verification, provided the
    deferral is stated and the work is not left uncommitted.

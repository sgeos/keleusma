COMPLETION CONDITION — the endianness obligation is located and ratcheted (V0.3.X)

Ordering is NOT a completion criterion. The properties below may be reached in
any order, by any route. Judge the end state of the tree and the transcript, not
the sequence, the branch, or the process used to get there.

DONE when all of the following hold.

1. WHO CHECKS AND WHO CHOOSES IS STATED SEPARATELY.
   The record says which component tests endianness and which component selects
   the target the code is emitted for, and does not imply they are the same.

2. THE GAP IS CLASSIFIED AS LIVE OR LATENT, WITH THE REASON.
   Whether the insufficiency is reachable today is stated, together with the
   condition that makes it reachable, rather than left as an unqualified caveat.

3. A CHANGE THAT WOULD MAKE THE OBLIGATION MOVE IS MADE TO FAIL LOUDLY.
   Something in the tree fails if the precondition behind the current
   arrangement stops holding, and it names the obligation rather than only
   reporting a difference.

4. THE RATCHET PROVES ITS REACH.
   It is exercised against a case where it must fail, so a pass is evidence
   about the tree rather than about the check. A check demonstrated only on
   cases it passes does not satisfy this.

5. NO CLAIM IS MADE THAT THE HAZARD IS REPAIRED.
   The record distinguishes locating and pinning an obligation from discharging
   it, and does not describe a ratchet as a fix.

6. WHAT CANNOT BE TESTED HERE IS SAID PLAINLY.
   Where a branch cannot execute on the machines this project builds on, that is
   stated as a limit on the evidence rather than passed over.

7. FIGURES CARRY THEIR POPULATION.
   Any count states what it ranges over and which check produced it.

8. NOTHING IS CLAIMED THAT WAS NOT RUN.
   Every count and pass or fail claim corresponds to output in the transcript.
   Carried figures are marked as carried.

9. A FAILURE IS REPORTED BEFORE IT IS REPAIRED.
   Any failing test is named with its cause and with a statement of whether that
   cause belongs to this line.

NOT completion criteria: which branch anything sits on, whether the work is
committed or pushed, how many commits were made, the order of the properties
above, or whether big-endian support is added.

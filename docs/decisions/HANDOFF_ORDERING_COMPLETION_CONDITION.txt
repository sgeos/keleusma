COMPLETION CONDITION — handoff ordering and superseded headings

Ordering is explicitly NOT a completion criterion. Any order satisfies this.

All of the following must hold as observable properties of the tree.

1. The resume document for this line states, without relying on a relative word
   like "later", which end of the document is authoritative when two sections
   disagree. A reader who knows only that rule can resolve a conflict correctly.

2. Every section heading whose factual claim has been superseded says so in the
   heading itself, and names or points to where the current statement lives. A
   reader skimming only headings does not collect a superseded figure.

3. The superseded sections are retained rather than deleted, because they record
   how a wrong conclusion was reached.

4. No section is renumbered or moved in a way that invalidates existing
   references within the document.

5. Every figure the document states as current matches a run, and its stated
   test count matches the suite.

6. The document does not claim to be short or easy to navigate. Any statement
   about its size is accurate.

7. The files recorded as owned by the other line are unmodified.

8. The native code generator's suite passes with zero failures and its
   formatting and lint checks pass, with the pass/fail judgement taken from
   something other than a truncated or piped view of the output.

9. Work that is finished is committed. Pushing may be deferred while a pull
   request of this line is mid-verification, provided the deferral is stated and
   the work is not left uncommitted.

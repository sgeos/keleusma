COMPLETION CONDITION — drift in prose

Ordering is NOT a completion criterion. Any order of the work satisfies this, and
nothing here requires a particular branch, commit shape, or process step.

All of the following hold in the tree:

1. THE ATTEMPT AT A GENERAL INSTRUMENT IS RECORDED WITH ITS MEASUREMENT.
   The tree states what was matched, how large the population was, what fraction
   of it was live status rather than narrative, and why that ruled the instrument
   out -- rather than reporting only that it was not built.

2. EVERY RECORD CORRECTED THIS SESSION IS EITHER GUARDED OR NAMED AS UNGUARDED.
   For each, the tree either contains something that fails if the underlying
   state reverts, or says plainly that nothing does.

3. ANY NEW GUARD IS SHOWN TO FIRE.
   A guard added here is demonstrated to fail when the state it watches is
   absent, not merely to pass.

4. THE RESIDUAL IS STATED.
   The tree says that prose drift is closed claim by claim and that no general
   mechanism exists, rather than implying the gap is closed.

5. NO CLAIM IS MADE THAT DATING PROSE WOULD SOLVE IT,
   or the reason it would not is given.

6. NO NEW OPCODE, NO BYTECODE_VERSION CHANGE, AND NO EDIT TO src/ OR tests/ AT
   THE REPOSITORY ROOT.

7. THE BACKEND SUITE IS GREEN IN BOTH FLOAT CONFIGURATIONS, with counts
   re-derived rather than quoted.

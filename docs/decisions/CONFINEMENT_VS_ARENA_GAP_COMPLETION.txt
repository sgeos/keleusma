COMPLETION CONDITION — joining confinement to the arena-bound gap

Ordering is NOT a completion criterion. These properties may be reached in any
order, in any number of commits, on any branch. Judge the end state of the tree,
never the sequence that produced it.

TREE-CHECKABLE END STATE

1. Something in the tree reports, for the modules whose backend arena demand
   exceeds their verified figure, how their construction sites are distributed
   across the confinement analysis's three answers. The three are reported
   separately; the inconclusive answer is never folded into the confident one.

2. That per-module picture is reported alongside the corpus-wide distribution,
   with the two clearly distinguished as different populations, so a reader can
   tell whether the exceeding modules differ from the corpus at all.

3. The report states explicitly what the measurement does NOT license. In
   particular it does not present any figure as an achievable or proposed arena
   bound, and it says why: confinement licenses reuse across scopes rather than
   within one, so a count of confined sites is not a demand.

4. The direction of soundness is stated: which of the reported counts is an
   upper bound and which a lower bound on what it stands for.

5. A verdict is recorded on whether confinement could plausibly account for the
   gap, and it is one the reported numbers support rather than one they merely
   suggest. If the numbers are inconclusive, that is the recorded verdict.

6. Any instrument used is demonstrated to discriminate — shown to produce
   different answers on inputs that should differ — rather than trusted because
   its output looked plausible.

7. No distribution over a population that ordinary corpus growth moves is
   pinned as an equality, unless the pin exists to announce that growth and says
   so.

8. Nothing in the code-generation path consumes a confinement verdict. The
   region planner still assigns per static site, so no verdict from this
   analysis can affect emitted code.

9. No file under src/selfhost/, src/confine.rs, and none of src/verify.rs,
   src/bytecode.rs, src/vm.rs, src/wire_schema.rs, or .github/workflows/,
   differs from the v0.2.3 commit the branch has absorbed.

10. Both suites are green, each verified by BOTH cargo's own exit status AND the
    summed per-binary counts, with the two agreeing:
      - the main workspace, run so a failure does not truncate the run
      - native_codegen, which continuous integration does not build and whose
        local run is therefore its only gate
    The two are not run concurrently with each other, because the workspace
    carries a wall-clock guard that concurrent load makes fail.

11. Every census and gate figure quoted as current was re-derived against the
    present tree after the last change capable of moving it.

12. The handoff validates against the tree: its ancestry checks pass, and its
    stated suite and census figures match a fresh run.

13. No count is presented as evidence without the command producing it being
    recoverable from the surrounding text.

NOT REQUIRED

- Closing the arena-bound gap, or proposing a bound.
- Wiring confinement into region planning.
- Resolving the Fixed shared-slot ABI or the float entry ABI; both are held by
  the operator.
- Any particular branch name, pull request, merge, or commit count.

DISQUALIFYING

- Presenting a count of confined sites as an achievable demand or bound.
- Folding the inconclusive verdict into the confident one.
- Reporting corpus-wide and exceeding-module figures as one population.
- A guard that cannot fail, or whose ability to fail was never shown.
- A green reached by narrowing a population or relaxing an assertion, unless
  what was excluded is still reported with its cause.
- A suite result read from a pipeline's exit status rather than the command's.

COMPLETION CONDITION — the checked fixed-point multiply

Ordering is NOT a completion criterion. Any order of the work satisfies this, and
nothing here requires a particular branch, commit shape, or process step.

All of the following hold in the tree:

1. A CHECKED FIXED-POINT MULTIPLY AGREES WITH THE REFERENCE.
   A program using the checked form of fixed-point multiplication -- the one with
   ok and overflow arms -- produces the same result natively as on the virtual
   machine, for an ordinary product, for one that exceeds the word range, and for
   one that falls below it.

2. THE THREE PUSHED SLOTS MATCH THE RUNTIME'S.
   The value, the second slot and the flag each carry what the reference puts
   there, established against the reference rather than from description. In
   particular the second slot is not the integer form's high half.

3. THE LOW SLOT WRAPS RATHER THAN SATURATES,
   matching the checked contract and distinct from the saturating bare form, and
   a test distinguishes the two.

4. AN OUT-OF-RANGE FRACTION COUNT IS STILL REFUSED,
   as the bare form already refuses it, rather than shifting by a count at or
   beyond the word width.

5. THE CHECKED DIVIDE IS STILL REFUSED, with a reason naming what it would
   require rather than being left unexplained.

6. THE TEST THAT ASSERTED THE MULTIPLY REFUSAL NOW ASSERTS AGREEMENT
   rather than having been deleted.

7. NEW CENSUS-TRACKED SITES ARE CLASSIFIED, and any upstream premise the
   lowering relies on is recorded where the census requires.

8. NO NEW OPCODE, NO BYTECODE_VERSION CHANGE, AND NO EDIT TO src/ OR tests/ AT
   THE REPOSITORY ROOT.

9. THE BACKEND SUITE IS GREEN IN BOTH FLOAT CONFIGURATIONS, with counts
   re-derived rather than quoted.

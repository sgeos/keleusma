COMPLETION CONDITION — the planner/verifier liveness axis

Ordering is NOT a completion criterion. These properties may be reached in any
order, in any number of commits, on any branch. Judge the end state of the tree,
never the sequence that produced it.

TREE-CHECKABLE END STATE

1. Something in the tree compares, for every module carrying a construction
   site, the syntactic path-maximum against the liveness figure implied by the
   verifier, and reports where the two agree and where they differ.

2. The two directions of disagreement are reported separately: the walk
   exceeding the verifier, and the walk falling below it. The second is
   identified as a defect in the walk rather than an interesting divergence, or
   shown not to occur.

3. Modules with no construction site are excluded, and the exclusion is stated.
   No figure is derived by dividing by a zero site count.

4. Where the two differ, the report gives enough context to tell whether the
   difference is explained by loop placement, and says whether it is a defect or
   a modelling difference.

5. A verdict is recorded on whether a planner could reproduce the verifier's
   figure, distinguishing what holds on the modules already examined from what
   holds across the whole corpus. Agreement is described as evidence about this
   corpus rather than as a general property.

6. The earlier claim that the two axes agreed was tested on a population where
   every site was a bare conditional. The report states whether that agreement
   survives on the population where it was not tested.

7. The instrument is demonstrated to discriminate rather than trusted because
   its output looked plausible, and it was not adjusted to improve agreement
   with the thing it is compared against.

8. Nothing in the code-generation path changed. The region planner still assigns
   per static site.

9. No file under src/, and none of src/selfhost/, src/confine.rs, src/verify.rs,
   src/bytecode.rs, src/vm.rs, src/wire_schema.rs, or .github/workflows/,
   differs from the v0.2.3 commit the branch has absorbed.

10. Both suites are green, each verified by BOTH cargo's own exit status AND the
    summed per-binary counts, with the two agreeing:
      - the main workspace, run so a failure does not truncate the run
      - native_codegen, which continuous integration does not build and whose
        local run is therefore its only gate
    The two are not run concurrently with each other, because the workspace
    carries a wall-clock guard that concurrent load makes fail.

11. Every census and gate figure quoted as current was re-derived against the
    present tree after the last change capable of moving it.

12. The handoff validates against the tree: its ancestry checks pass, and its
    stated suite and census figures match a fresh run.

13. No count is presented as evidence without the command producing it being
    recoverable from the surrounding text.

NOT REQUIRED

- Agreement. A divergence, with where and why it occurs, is a result — and one
  that would weaken an earlier finding, which is a reason to report it rather
  than to avoid it.
- Adopting the remedy or establishing its remaining conditions.
- Resolving the Fixed shared-slot ABI or the float entry ABI; both are held by
  the operator.
- Any particular branch name, pull request, merge, or commit count.

DISQUALIFYING

- Tuning the walk until it agrees with the verifier, rather than reporting the
  disagreement.
- Presenting corpus-wide agreement as proof that a planner can reproduce the
  verifier in general.
- Collapsing the two directions of disagreement into one count.
- A guard that cannot fail, or whose ability to fail was never shown.
- A green reached by excluding modules whose divergence is inconvenient, unless
  the exclusion and its cause are reported.
- A suite result read from a pipeline's exit status rather than the command's.

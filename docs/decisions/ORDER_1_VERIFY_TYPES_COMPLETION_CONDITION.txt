COMPLETION CONDITION — seeding the type-verification stage

Ordering is explicitly NOT a completion criterion. Any order satisfies this.

All of the following must hold as observable properties of the tree.

1. The type-verification self-hosted stage is driven from more than one
   subject, or the tree records why it cannot be and names the
   obstacle.

2. Each driven subject is shown to reach its verdict within the drive
   budget, and to move that verdict. A subject that runs without
   reaching or moving its verdict is reported rather than counted as
   agreement.

3. The tree states why the subjects are sized as they are, referring to
   the stage's own published step bound rather than leaving the size
   unexplained.

4. Shared slots are resolved by name through the module layout, never
   by a computed or assumed index.

5. The report states how many stages are seeded and names those still
   unseeded, and does not imply the unseeded set is closed.

6. Files owned by the other line are unmodified. Defects found in them
   are recorded as reports, including any documented host-facing
   channel that nothing reads, and any discrepancy between a stage's
   published step bound and the budget it is driven with.

7. Any stage that disagrees on a newly added subject is reported as a
   finding and investigated, not exempted or masked.

8. The tree does not claim the Order-1 gate is met.

9. Any claim that a figure changed is supported by a recorded
   measurement of both the before and after values.

10. The native code generator's suite passes with zero failures and its
    formatting and lint checks pass, with the pass/fail judgement taken
    from something other than a truncated or piped view of the output.

11. Work that is finished is committed. Pushing may be deferred while a
    pull request of this line is mid-verification, provided the
    deferral is stated and the work is not left uncommitted.

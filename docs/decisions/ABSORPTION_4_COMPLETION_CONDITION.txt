COMPLETION CONDITION — absorption 4

Ordering is explicitly NOT a completion criterion. Any order satisfies this.

All of the following must hold as observable properties of the tree.

1. The other line's work is absorbed into this line at the commit the
   tree records, and the tree names which commit that is.

2. Files owned by the other line are byte-identical to that absorbed
   commit. The comparison is made against the absorbed commit, not
   against a branch tip that continues to move.

3. Every assertion that failed during absorption is rewritten to state
   the new truth. None is deleted or weakened to make the tree green,
   and what each previously asserted remains recorded.

4. Every figure the resume document states about the self-hosted stage
   gate is produced by a run on the absorbed tree. Figures are not
   carried forward from before the absorption.

5. Whether the stage-gate figures moved is stated as a measurement.
   A passing suite alone is not offered as evidence that they did not.

6. Any stage seed that stops building, or any subject that stops
   reaching its verdict, is reported with its cause named rather than
   absorbed into a smaller count.

7. The record of which commit ownership is checked against is updated
   to the new absorption point wherever it is stated.

8. The tree does not claim the stage gate is met.

9. The native code generator's suite passes with zero failures and its
   formatting and lint checks pass, with the pass/fail judgement taken
   from something other than a truncated or piped view of the output.

10. Work that is finished is committed. Pushing may be deferred while a
    pull request of this line is mid-verification, provided the
    deferral is stated and the work is not left uncommitted.

COMPLETION CONDITION — re-deriving the lowering coverage and its recommendation

Ordering is NOT a completion criterion. These properties may be reached in any
order, in any number of commits, on any branch. Judge the end state of the tree,
never the sequence that produced it.

TREE-CHECKABLE END STATE

1. The document that recommends a next increment for native lowering states
   coverage figures re-derived against the present tree, and its recommendation
   is consistent with them. A recommendation naming a workstream that the
   present measurement shows blocking nothing does not stand unqualified.

2. Where a figure was superseded, the earlier text is visible rather than
   deleted, and what changed is stated, so a reader meets the correction rather
   than a silent edit.

3. Figures given at different granularities are distinguished. A chunk-level
   coverage number and a module-level one are not presented as the same measure,
   and the relationship between them is stated.

4. Any comparison between an old and a new figure is over the same measure, and
   the fact that the underlying corpus changed size is stated rather than left
   for the reader to notice.

5. The modules or chunks that remain unlowerable are named, with the cause for
   each, rather than summarised only as a count.

6. No claim is made that the backend is complete. Remaining refusals are
   reported, including those with established reasons of their own.

7. No coverage figure is pinned as an equality that ordinary corpus growth would
   move; figures are dated instead.

8. Nothing in the backend's behaviour changed, and no workstream was started on
   the strength of the superseded recommendation.

9. No file under src/, and none of src/selfhost/, src/confine.rs, src/verify.rs,
   src/bytecode.rs, src/vm.rs, src/wire_schema.rs, or .github/workflows/,
   differs from the v0.2.3 commit the branch has absorbed.

10. Both suites are green, each verified by BOTH cargo's own exit status AND the
    summed per-binary counts, with the two agreeing:
      - the main workspace, run so a failure does not truncate the run
      - native_codegen, which continuous integration does not build and whose
        local run is therefore its only gate
    The two are not run concurrently with each other, because the workspace
    carries a wall-clock guard that concurrent load makes fail.

11. The handoff validates against the tree: its ancestry checks pass, and its
    stated suite and census figures match a fresh run.

12. No count is presented as evidence without the command producing it being
    recoverable from the surrounding text.

NOT REQUIRED

- Choosing a replacement next increment. Establishing that the recorded one is
  obsolete, and naming what actually remains, is the goal; deciding what to do
  about it is separate.
- Lowering any remaining refused opcode or module.
- Resolving the Fixed shared-slot ABI or the float entry ABI; both are held by
  the operator.
- Any particular branch name, pull request, merge, or commit count.

DISQUALIFYING

- Leaving a recommendation standing that the present measurement contradicts.
- Comparing an old figure to a new one at a different granularity, or without
  noting the corpus changed size.
- Describing the backend as complete or the remaining refusals as absent.
- Deleting a superseded figure instead of correcting it visibly.
- Pinning a coverage figure that corpus growth would move.
- A suite result read from a pipeline's exit status rather than the command's.

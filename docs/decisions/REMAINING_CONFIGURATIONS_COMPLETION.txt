   Wherever the branch was described as verified under one configuration only,
   that description now reflects what has been run.

   If the outcome matches the prediction, the record says it confirms an
   expectation rather than resolving a doubt.

   Every count and pass/fail claim corresponds to output appearing in the
   transcript. Carried figures are marked as carried.

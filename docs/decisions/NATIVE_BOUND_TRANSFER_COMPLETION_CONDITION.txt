COMPLETION CONDITION — native bound transfer (Workstream E)

Ordering is explicitly NOT a completion criterion. Any order satisfies this.

All of the following must hold as observable properties of the tree.

1. A test in the native code generator compares, over the real corpus, the
   backend's operand-stack provisioning against the per-chunk figure the
   bytecode verifier computes. It reports the MAXIMUM observed requirement and
   the headroom against the provisioning, not merely a pass or fail.

2. A test compares, over the real corpus, the backend's composite-region byte
   demand against the heap figure the bytecode verifier computes for the same
   module. Whether the demand is bounded by the verified figure is stated
   explicitly as a result, in whichever direction the measurement went.

3. Both comparisons assert a non-trivial denominator: the number of chunks or
   modules actually compared is asserted to be substantial and is printed, so a
   comparison over an empty or tiny set fails rather than passes.

4. Units are not conflated. Any place a slot count and a byte count are brought
   together states the conversion explicitly, or the two are kept separate.

5. The backend's operand-stack-too-deep refusal is either demonstrated by a
   test that constructs a subject reaching it, or the tree records that nothing
   reaches it and that an untriggered guard is not a demonstrated guard.

6. At least one of the new comparisons is shown to be load-bearing by a recorded
   mutation: the transcript states what was changed, that the changed version
   produced a different result or a failure, and that the change was reverted
   with the revert verified.

7. Any margin reported is accompanied by its denominator and by an explicit
   statement that a comfortable margin over this corpus is not a proof of the
   general property.

8. If a violation is found, it is named precisely and is NOT resolved by
   widening the provisioning or by relaxing an assertion. If no violation is
   found, the tree does not claim the bound is proven.

9. The resume document for this line records the outcome with every figure taken
   from a run rather than carried forward, and its stated test count matches the
   suite.

10. The files recorded as read-only for this line are unmodified: the bytecode
    verifier, the virtual machine, the bytecode definitions, the self-hosted
    compiler sources, and the continuous-integration workflows.

11. The native code generator's own suite passes with zero failures, and its
    formatting and lint checks pass, with the pass/fail judgement taken from
    something other than a truncated or piped view of the output.

12. Work that is finished is committed and pushed. Nothing required by the
    points above is left only in the working tree.

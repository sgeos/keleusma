ORDERING IS NOT A COMPLETION CRITERION. The properties below may be reached in
any order, in any number of commits, on any branch. Nothing here requires a
particular sequence, commit message, or process step.

Checked against the worktree holding branch v0.3.0.

1. THE SHARED-SEGMENT CANARY
- Either a resumable stream that writes a shared data slot is driven on BOTH
  implementations and their yielded sequences are compared, OR a document in the
  tree records, citing the measurement that showed it, why such a stream cannot
  be driven.
- If such a stream is driven, the tree records that the shared-buffer overrun
  check was demonstrated to FIRE when the buffer is undersized — reach shown, not
  asserted.
- No file still describes the shared canary as having unproven reach if that
  reach has since been proven, and none claims proven reach without the
  demonstration above.

2. THE TWO NATIVE-CALL OPCODES
- Either each of the two native-call opcodes has a witness program that emits it,
  executes on both implementations, and agrees on the result, OR the tree records
  why one or both cannot have such a witness, with the obstruction named
  concretely.
- Any recorded count of driven opcode witnesses agrees with what the tree
  actually drives.
- A witness registers its native on both sides; nothing compares a program only
  against itself.

3. HONEST INSTRUMENTS
- Any guard whose pinned figure changed has the change explained in the tree, not
  merely the constant edited.
- No test added here passes while exercising nothing: each either asserts
  agreement between the two implementations or demonstrably fails when its
  subject is perturbed.

4. THE SUITE
- native_codegen is clean under formatting and under lints as errors, and its
  suite passes in both float configurations.
- Any test that does not pass is recorded as a finding rather than deleted,
  skipped, or weakened.

5. RECORDS
- The handoff for this line has state-table figures agreeing with the tree, and
  its figure guard passes.
- Claims made stale by this work are corrected where they stand.

6. TREE STATE
- The working tree is clean and the branch's commits are on origin.

COMPLETION CONDITION — the disposition of every unlowered opcode (V0.3.X)

Ordering is NOT a completion criterion. The properties below may be reached in
any order, by any route. Judge the end state of the tree and the transcript, not
the sequence, the branch, or the process used to get there.

DONE when all of the following hold.

1. EVERY OPCODE OUTSIDE THE LOWERED SET CARRIES A DISPOSITION.
   For each one, the tree records which of these it is: support is missing;
   refusal is deliberate and correct; accepted by a route the census does not
   instrument; or no verdict is available because nothing exercises it.

2. A DISPOSITION IS BACKED BY EVIDENCE, NOT BY A READING OF INTENT.
   Each disposition points at something that was run, or at a named constraint
   that makes the disposition correct. A comment asserting a reason is not by
   itself evidence.

3. A DELIBERATE REFUSAL IS DISTINGUISHED FROM AN UNIMPLEMENTED ONE.
   Where refusing is the correct behaviour, the record says why refusing is
   correct rather than leaving it to read as an unfinished feature.

4. THE HEADLINE FRACTION CANNOT BE READ AS A COUNT OF MISSING SUPPORT.
   Wherever a count of lowered opcodes is reported, a reader is not left to infer
   that the remainder are unsupported. The census output itself carries this,
   not only prose elsewhere.

5. A DISPOSITION THAT DEPENDS ON ANOTHER LINE'S CODE SAYS SO.
   Where the disposition rests on behaviour in files this line does not own, that
   ownership and its consequence are stated.

6. GUARDS ADDED HERE PROVE THEIR REACH.
   Any check introduced is exercised against a case where it must fail. A check
   demonstrated only on a case it passes does not satisfy this. Where a check
   asserts a negative, the instrument behind it is shown capable of reporting the
   positive.

7. FIGURES CARRY THEIR POPULATION.
   Any count states which census produced it and which population that census
   reads, and complementary censuses are not presented as one.

8. NOTHING IS CLAIMED THAT WAS NOT RUN.
   Every count and every disposition corresponds to output appearing in the
   transcript. Figures carried from an earlier measurement are marked as carried.

9. A FAILURE IS REPORTED BEFORE IT IS REPAIRED.
   Any failing test or refused compilation is named with its cause, and with a
   statement of whether that cause belongs to this line.

NOT completion criteria: which branch anything sits on, whether the work is
committed, how many commits were made, the order of the properties above, how
many opcodes change disposition, or whether any other goal in the same session
advanced.

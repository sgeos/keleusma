ORDERING IS NOT A COMPLETION CRITERION. The properties below may be reached in
any order, in any number of commits. Nothing here requires a particular branch,
commit message, or sequence of steps.

All properties are checked against the worktree holding branch v0.3.0.

1. ABSORPTION
- The unabsorbed count from origin/v0.2.3 is zero, OR a document under
  docs/decisions/ names the commits left unabsorbed and why.
- A brief for absorption 62 exists under docs/decisions/ carrying BOTH the
  commit it was computed against AND the unabsorbed count at that moment.
- That brief carries an outcome that scores every prediction it made, figures
  and guards alike, as held or failed. A prediction that failed is recorded as
  failed with its reason; it is not edited into a hit.

2. THE ARENA MEASUREMENT
- A test under native_codegen/tests/ measures the touched extent of the arena
  region for at least one resumable stream and asserts a relationship between
  that extent at a low tick count and at a high one.
- The measurement is not defeated by a written byte coinciding with a fill
  value.
- It carries a non-vacuity check: the measured extent is greater than zero.
- It carries a discrimination check: subjects with different arena appetite
  produce different extents; OR the file records, citing the measurement that
  showed it, that no such pair is available.
- The file states plainly that its result is evidence about the shapes and tick
  counts driven, and is not a static bound.
- If growth was found, or if the lowered program's output was found to depend on
  how the arena buffer is initialised, that finding is recorded in the tree. It
  is not worked around by changing the probe until it passes.

3. THE SUITE
- native_codegen is clean under formatting and under lints as errors, and its
  suite passes in both float configurations.
- Any test that does not pass is recorded as a finding rather than deleted,
  skipped, or weakened.
- No guard's pinned constant is changed without the change that moved it being
  described in the tree.

4. RECORDS THAT MUST NOT OUTLIVE THEIR SUBJECTS
- The handoff for this line has state-table figures that agree with the tree,
  and its figure guard passes.
- No file still asserts that nothing on this line measures whether the arena
  grows across ticks, if such a measurement now exists.
- Any open report this line holds against the other line still reproduces, or is
  retracted.

5. TREE STATE
- The working tree is clean and the branch's commits are on origin.

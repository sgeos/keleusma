Ordering is NOT a completion criterion. Any sequence, any number of
commits, any branch layout satisfies this. Judge only the end state of
the tree.

All of the following must hold.

1. THE BARE LOOP FORM APPEARS IN THE CONSTRUCT-SUPPORT BOUNDARY.
   The table that classifies constructs as supported, refused,
   divergent, or reference-rejected contains a case for the bare loop
   form, distinguishable from the counted form both by the classifier
   and by a reader scanning the table as text.

2. ITS CLASSIFICATION IS DERIVED, NOT ASSERTED.
   The recorded classification is what the classifier actually produces
   for that case, and the boundary's per-category counts match the tree.
   No count is written by inference from a previous count.

3. THE COUNTS AGREE EVERYWHERE THEY ARE RECORDED.
   Wherever the boundary's category counts appear, they are the same
   numbers and they match what the tree produces. A count updated in one
   place and not another does not satisfy this.

4. THE GAP PIN'S SUBJECT IS RESOLVED, NOT ERASED.
   The test asserting the boundary carried no such case is written to
   fail when that stops being true. It is not deleted silently: either
   it now asserts what became of what it watched, or its removal is
   accompanied by a statement of what replaced it and where.

5. THE COUNTED FORM STILL PASSES, AND IS PART OF THE EVIDENCE.
   The existing loop case still classifies as supported. Without it, "the
   bare form is refused" would be satisfied by a compiler that refuses
   every loop.

6. THE REFUSAL IS STILL BY NAME.
   Whatever the boundary records, the refusal a user receives still
   identifies the construct and the supported alternative. A boundary
   entry does not substitute for that.

7. NOTHING PREVIOUSLY ACCEPTED IS NOW REJECTED.
   No construct's classification changes except by a stated reason. No
   behaviour changes. No opcode. No BYTECODE_VERSION change.

8. VERIFICATION IS GREEN AND REPORTED HONESTLY.
   The test suite passes with zero failures under default features and
   under the self-host feature, formatting is clean, clippy is clean at
   deny-warnings, and the workspace documentation builds without
   warnings. The suite's own exit status is read rather than a
   pipeline's, and the run does not stop at the first failing binary.
   Any step skipped or goal abandoned is stated plainly with its reason.

9. BLOCKERS ARE RECORDED, NOT WORKED AROUND.
   If any item proved impossible without an operator decision, the tree
   names the decision required and the evidence establishing it, and the
   remaining items still satisfy their conditions.

THIS DOES NOT REQUIRE SUPPORTING THE CONSTRUCT. The bare form compiles
to a materially different and much smaller instruction sequence than the
counted form, so support is a second lowering across several stage
sources. Recording the gap in the inventory is the whole of what is
asked; claiming support that was not built would be worse than a
smaller true result.

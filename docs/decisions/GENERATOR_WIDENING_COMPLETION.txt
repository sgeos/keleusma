ORDERING IS NOT A COMPLETION CRITERION. The properties below may be reached in
any order, in any number of commits, on any branch. Nothing here requires a
particular sequence, commit message, or process step.

Checked against the worktree holding branch v0.3.0.

1. THE GENERATOR IS WIDER AND STILL BOUNDED
- Generated programs are compared across more than one seed, and across at least
  one operand type beyond the one originally covered.
- The number of distinct programs compared is asserted against a floor that
  reflects the widening.
- Depth or magnitude limits are derived per operand type rather than shared, so
  that checked arithmetic cannot overflow for any type generated.
- The added runtime is stated and remains small relative to the gate's longest
  phase.

2. REACH IS SHOWN FOR THE NEW COVERAGE
- The tree records a perturbation of the lowering that the newly added coverage
  catches, distinct from the perturbation used for the original coverage.
- If a perturbation for the new type could not be made to fire, that is recorded
  as a finding rather than omitted.

3. THE SCOPE IS STATED
- The tree says which operand types the generator does not emit and why, in terms
  a reader can disagree with.
- A green run is not described as a correctness claim about the lowering.

4. FINDINGS
- Any divergence is recorded, reduced toward a minimal case, and described as the
  two implementations disagreeing rather than as a verdict about which is wrong.
- Nothing is weakened, skipped or deleted to make a run green.

5. THE SUITE
- native_codegen is clean under formatting and under lints as errors, and its
  suite passes in both float configurations.
- Any guard whose pinned figure changed has the change explained in the tree.

6. RECORDS AND TREE STATE
- The handoff for this line has state-table figures agreeing with the tree, and
  its figure guard passes.
- The working tree is clean and the branch's commits are on origin.

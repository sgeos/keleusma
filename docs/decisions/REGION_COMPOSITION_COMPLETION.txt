ORDERING IS NOT A COMPLETION CRITERION. The properties below may be reached in
any order, in any number of commits, on any branch. Nothing here requires a
particular sequence, commit message, or process step.

Checked against the worktree holding branch v0.3.0.

1. THE CENSUS
- A test under native_codegen/tests/ decomposes the backend's published host
  arena figure into its constituent planner terms for every corpus module that
  has an entry point, not for a hand-picked subset.
- The census asserts that the named terms account for the published figure: they
  do not exceed it, and any unexplained remainder is bounded by a stated
  threshold with the reason for that threshold given.
- Modules with a stream chunk and modules without are reported separately, since
  the spill reservation applies only to the former.
- The population size is asserted to be non-trivial, so a census that enumerated
  nothing cannot pass.
- A figure the census pins fails when it moves, and the failure message asks for
  a classification rather than a new constant.

2. THE RESULT IS RECORDED HONESTLY
- The tree states what fraction of the published figure is the flat spill
  reservation, across the corpus rather than across the three shapes that
  motivated the question.
- The record says plainly that over-provisioning is safe and that the
  reservation's stated rationale stands; the finding is the size of the
  looseness, not its existence.
- If the decomposition does not account for the published figure, that is
  recorded as the result rather than worked around.

3. NO PLANNER CHANGE
- The region planner's behaviour is unchanged by this work. If any change to it
  is made, the reason is recorded and the open soundness obligation against it is
  addressed explicitly.

4. THE SHARED RECORD
- The project-level catalogue of ways a green verification run has under-reported
  includes the case where a gate run phase by phase has its verdict assembled by
  hand, so a phase can be omitted without anything saying so.

5. THE SUITE
- native_codegen is clean under formatting and under lints as errors, and its
  suite passes in both float configurations.
- Any test that does not pass is recorded as a finding rather than deleted,
  skipped, or weakened.
- Any guard whose pinned figure changed has the change explained in the tree.

6. RECORDS AND TREE STATE
- The handoff for this line has state-table figures agreeing with the tree, and
  its figure guard passes.
- The working tree is clean and the branch's commits are on origin.

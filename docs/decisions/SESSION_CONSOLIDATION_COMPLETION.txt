ORDERING IS NOT A COMPLETION CRITERION. The properties below may be reached in
any order, in any number of commits, on any branch. Nothing here requires a
particular sequence, commit message, or process step.

Checked against the worktree holding branch v0.3.0.

1. THE HANDOFF DESCRIBES THE TREE
- Its newest session block covers the work most recently landed, including the
  absorption, the arena measurements, the outgoing disclosure, and the optimiser
  work.
- Its state-table figures agree with the tree and its figure guard passes.
- It does not gain an additional section claiming to be the place to resume; the
  newest block is identifiable without ambiguity.

2. VALIDITY IS RUNNABLE AND RUN
- The handoff's ancestry block includes an anchor for this session's work, and
  every anchor in it is an ancestor of the branch head when run.
- The result of running it is stated, with the anchor count derived from the
  block rather than quoted from prose.

3. WHAT IS UNFINISHED IS VISIBLE
- The tree names, in one place a resuming reader will reach, what remains open
  and who it depends on: the reports outstanding with the other line, the
  question outstanding with the operator, and any measurement deliberately
  declined together with its recorded reasoning.
- Nothing is described as complete that is blocked, and nothing is described as
  blocked that this line could finish alone.

4. CLAIMS MATCH THE TREE
- No commit message or document asserts a change the tree does not contain.
- Any guard whose pinned figure changed has the change explained.

5. THE SUITE
- native_codegen is clean under formatting and under lints as errors, and its
  suite passes in both float configurations.
- Any test that does not pass is recorded as a finding rather than deleted,
  skipped, or weakened.

6. TREE STATE
- The working tree is clean and the branch's commits are on origin.

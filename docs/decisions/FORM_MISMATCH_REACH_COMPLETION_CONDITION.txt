COMPLETION CONDITION — form-mismatch reachability

Ordering is explicitly NOT a completion criterion. Any order satisfies this.

All of the following must hold as observable properties of the tree.

1. Each of the four body-form-mismatch refusal sites carries either a
   demonstrated reachability across the full chain, or a bounded search recorded
   with the probes listed and the number that compiled stated.

2. The earlier wording that these sites "suggest unreachability" is replaced by
   whichever of the above the measurement supports. A suggestion is not left
   standing as a verdict.

3. The tree states how the body form and the operand form are each chosen, since
   the verdict rests on whether anything can decide them twice.

4. Probes are derived from the corpus or from the code that chooses the form, and
   any probe that fails to compile is counted separately from one that compiles
   and does not reach a site.

5. No claim of unreachability is made. Where nothing reached a site, the record
   says what was searched.

6. If any probe reaches a site, it is reported as a defect to the line that owns
   the compiler and the virtual machine, and the tree records the report.

7. The compiler, virtual machine, bytecode verifier, self-hosted compiler
   sources, and continuous-integration workflows are unmodified.

8. Every figure stated in the resume document for this line matches a run, and
   its stated test count matches the suite.

9. The native code generator's suite passes with zero failures and its
   formatting and lint checks pass, with the pass/fail judgement taken from
   something other than a truncated or piped view of the output.

10. Work that is finished is committed and pushed. Nothing required above is
    left only in the working tree.

COMPLETION CONDITION — arena shortfall cause

Ordering is explicitly NOT a completion criterion. Any order satisfies this.

All of the following must hold as observable properties of the tree.

1. The tree states the structural relation between the backend's
   region demand and the verified heap figure, and supports it with
   per-module arithmetic taken from a run: the number of composite
   construction sites, the size of each, and the two figures.

2. The earlier reading that the shortfall is a fixed per-entity
   quantum is corrected wherever it was published, including any
   summary table and any operator-facing decision list. No location
   still asserts it.

3. The tree states that the common divisor is a property of this
   corpus rather than evidence of a unit, and shows at least one
   module whose composite size differs from that divisor.

4. The tree states that the gap grows with the number of static
   composite sites rather than sitting at a fixed overhead, so the
   observed multiple is a property of this corpus and not a ceiling.

5. The byte magnitudes already reported are retained and remain
   accurate. Only the interpretation attached to them changes.

6. The tree does not describe the backend's allocation strategy as a
   defect, and states that the two figures measure different things.

7. The tree makes no recommendation about how to close the gap, and
   continues to record that decision as the operator's.

8. Any claim about the relation states what it was derived from and
   what it did not read, so a reader can tell a measured fit from a
   proof.

9. Files owned by the other line are unmodified, checked against the
   commit this line last absorbed rather than a moving branch tip.

10. The native code generator's suite passes with zero failures and its
    formatting and lint checks pass, with the pass/fail judgement taken
    from something other than a truncated or piped view of the output.

11. Work that is finished is committed. Pushing may be deferred while a
    pull request of this line is mid-verification, provided the
    deferral is stated and the work is not left uncommitted.

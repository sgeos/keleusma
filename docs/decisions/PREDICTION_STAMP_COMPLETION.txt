COMPLETION CONDITION — the absorption prediction stamp

Ordering is NOT a completion criterion. Any order of the work satisfies this, and
nothing here requires a particular branch, commit shape, or process step.

All of the following hold in the tree:

1. THE RULE IS WRITTEN WHERE ITS SIBLINGS LIVE.
   The requirement that an absorption prediction record the backlog and commit it
   was computed against appears alongside the other absorption rules, not in a
   new competing document.

2. THE NEWEST ABSORPTION BRIEF CARRIES THE STAMP.
   It states the number of unabsorbed commits and the commit its prediction was
   computed against.

3. SOMETHING FAILS WHEN THE NEWEST ONE DOES NOT.
   A check in the tree locates the most recent absorption brief and fails if it
   lacks the stamp, with a message explaining that a prediction inherits the tree
   it was computed against.

4. THE CHECK IS HONEST ABOUT ITS SCOPE.
   It does not demand the stamp from briefs written before the rule existed, and
   it does not claim to verify that the recorded figures are correct -- only that
   they were recorded.

5. IT IS NON-VACUOUS.
   The check is shown to actually locate a brief rather than passing because it
   found none.

6. NO EDIT TO src/ OR tests/ AT THE REPOSITORY ROOT.

7. THE BACKEND SUITE IS GREEN IN BOTH FLOAT CONFIGURATIONS, with counts
   re-derived rather than quoted.

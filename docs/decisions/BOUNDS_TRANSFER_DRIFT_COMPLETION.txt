COMPLETION CONDITION — the bounds-transfer figures, pinned

Ordering is NOT a completion criterion. Any order of the work satisfies this, and
nothing here requires a particular branch, commit shape, or process step.

All of the following hold in the tree:

1. THE SPIKE ASSERTS ITS CONCLUSION.
   The bounds-transfer measurement fails if no inversion is found, so the claim
   it exists to support cannot silently become unsupported. It is non-vacuous:
   it also fails if it finds too few subjects to compare.

2. THE FIGURES ARE PINNED AND THEIR MOVEMENT IS VISIBLE.
   The population and inversion count are recorded in the tree, and a change in
   them causes a failure that asks for the cause to be established rather than
   for the number to be edited.

3. THE RECORDED DECISION DOCUMENT CARRIES THE RE-DERIVED FIGURES WITH A DATE,
   and the earlier figures remain visible as the dated record they are rather
   than being overwritten.

4. THE CAUSE OF THE MOVEMENT IS MEASURED OR EXPLICITLY LEFT OPEN.
   Any claim that the change came from corpus growth, from this line's own
   lowering changes, or from anything else is supported by a measurement. Where
   no measurement was made, the document says so instead of offering a plausible
   story.

5. THE SUBJECT OF THE MEASUREMENT IS UNCHANGED.
   The set being compared is the same kind of set as the one that produced the
   recorded figures, so the two are comparable.

6. NO NEW OPCODE, NO BYTECODE_VERSION CHANGE, AND NO EDIT TO src/ OR tests/ AT
   THE REPOSITORY ROOT.

7. THE BACKEND SUITE IS GREEN IN BOTH FLOAT CONFIGURATIONS, with counts
   re-derived rather than quoted.

COMPLETION CONDITION — the unexercised lowering arms are named

Ordering is NOT a completion criterion. No particular sequence of steps, branch,
commit shape, or process is required. Judge the end state only.

Complete when ALL of the following hold.

1. THE ARMS ARE ENUMERATED AT KIND GRANULARITY
   The tree records, for the lowering paths that branch on a scalar kind, which
   (opcode, kind) combinations the backend distinguishes. Opcode-level coverage
   alone does not satisfy this, because it cannot express an opcode whose one
   kind arm is unwitnessed.

2. CORPUS REACH IS MEASURED PER COMBINATION
   For each such combination the tree records whether the corpus produces it,
   measured from compiled modules rather than from parsing corpus or tooling
   source text.

3. EVERY UNREACHED COMBINATION IS RESOLVED
   Each combination the corpus does not reach is either attributed to a named
   test that exercises it, or recorded as unexercised. No combination is left
   unaccounted for, and "unexercised" appears where it is true rather than being
   quietly omitted.

4. THE DISTINCTION IS KEPT
   "The corpus does not reach it" and "nothing exercises it" are recorded as
   different claims. A document that treats them as the same does not satisfy
   this.

5. POPULATIONS ARE STATED
   Every count says what set it is over.

6. THE INSTRUMENT'S REACH IS ESTABLISHED
   The measurement demonstrates it is capable of reporting a non-empty or
   uncovered result, so a clean sweep cannot be mistaken for a clean tree.

7. NO SILENT SCOPE CHANGE
   If any gap is closed as part of this work rather than only recorded, that is
   stated plainly with what changed. If gaps remain open, no document claims or
   implies they are covered.

8. NOTHING AMBIGUOUS WAS DECIDED
   The open operator questions stay untouched: Fixed, Text, Opaque and Unit
   shared-slot representations remain unimplemented, and the region planner's
   soundness obligation is not discharged or restated as discharged.

9. THE SUITE IS GREEN AND THE COUNT RECONCILES
   The native_codegen suite passes with zero failures, read from cargo's own
   exit status AND from the summed per-binary counts, with the two agreeing and
   the binary count matching expectation. The total is accounted for as the
   previous total plus exactly the tests added.

10. QUALITY GATES
    Formatting, clippy at deny-warnings over tests as well as the library, and
    the documentation build at deny-warnings are all clean.

11. NO DEAD CITATIONS AND NO EXPIRED CLAIMS
    Nothing added or changed names a test, file, function, or identifier that
    does not exist, and no document left in the tree asserts a state that this
    work has made false.

If any item cannot be satisfied, say so explicitly with what was measured and
why it blocks, and satisfy every other item in full.

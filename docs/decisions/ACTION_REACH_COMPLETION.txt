COMPLETION CONDITION — reach discipline for actions, not only observations (V0.3.X)

Ordering is NOT a completion criterion. The properties below may be reached in
any order, by any route. Judge the end state of the tree and the transcript, not
the sequence, the branch, or the process used to get there.

DONE when all of the following hold.

1. THE DISTINCTION BETWEEN AN OVER-REACHING CHECK AND AN OVER-REACHING ACTION
   IS RECORDED.
   The record states that this project's reach discipline had been applied to
   things that observe and not to things that change or destroy, and says why
   the second needs it more: a check with excess reach reports something false,
   an action with excess reach destroys something real.

2. THE MECHANISM IS NAMED, NOT ONLY THE INCIDENT.
   The record says that selecting a process by program name is inherently
   machine-wide, and that the scoping property must come from something that
   distinguishes this checkout from a sibling.

3. VERIFICATION AFTER A DESTRUCTIVE ACTION IS ADDRESSED SEPARATELY.
   The record states that confirmation must rest on a signal the damage itself
   could not produce, and gives the concrete case where an orphaned process was
   read as evidence of a healthy run.

4. THE RULE IS STATED SO IT COVERS FUTURE INSTANCES, NOT THIS ONE.
   It is phrased as a class of action rather than as a prohibition on the single
   command that caused the incident.

5. THE COST IS STATED PLAINLY AND ATTRIBUTED.
   What was destroyed, whose it was, and that it happened while attempting to
   yield resources to its owner. No framing that distributes the cause.

6. A RECORD THAT ALREADY COVERED IT IS ACKNOWLEDGED IF ONE EXISTS.
   If a prior document or comment already described the correct scoping, that is
   named, along with the fact that it was not consulted.

7. NOTHING IS CLAIMED THAT WAS NOT RUN OR OBSERVED.
   Every process, load, or state claim corresponds to output in the transcript.
   Figures carried from earlier are marked as carried.

8. NO CLEANUP CLAIM RESTS ON ABSENCE ALONE.
   Any statement that the machine or a worktree is clear identifies what was
   checked and what such a check cannot see.

NOT completion criteria: which branch anything sits on, whether the work is
committed or pushed, how many commits were made, the order of the properties
above, or whether any other goal advanced.

COMPLETION CONDITION — the unexercised kind arms

Ordering is NOT a completion criterion. Any order of the work satisfies this, and
nothing here requires a particular branch, commit shape, or process step.

All of the following hold in the tree:

1. THE ACCEPTED-BUT-UNDRIVEN COMBINATION IS DRIVEN.
   A shared data slot of the scalar kind that lowers without being exercised is
   read through both the reference and the native code, over the same bytes, and
   the results agree.

2. THE CENSUS DISTINGUISHES WHY A COMBINATION IS UNREACHED.
   Its report separates combinations that cannot be constructed, combinations
   that are refused, and combinations that lower but nothing drives -- rather
   than reporting one label for all three. What was measured to place each is
   recorded.

3. THE DISTINCTION IS BACKED BY MEASUREMENT, NOT ASSERTION.
   For each combination called unconstructible or refused, the tree records the
   observation that establishes it.

4. THE STALE REFUSAL CLAIM IS CORRECTED WHERE IT IS MADE,
   and the ABI gap that file genuinely pins -- that a host cannot recover the
   fixed-point scale from the layout -- is preserved rather than retracted with
   it.

5. NOTHING IS MARKED COVERED THAT IS NOT DRIVEN.

6. NO NEW OPCODE, NO BYTECODE_VERSION CHANGE, AND NO EDIT TO src/ OR tests/ AT
   THE REPOSITORY ROOT.

7. THE BACKEND SUITE IS GREEN IN BOTH FLOAT CONFIGURATIONS, with counts
   re-derived rather than quoted.

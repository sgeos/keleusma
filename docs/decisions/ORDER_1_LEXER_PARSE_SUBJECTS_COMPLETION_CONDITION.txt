COMPLETION CONDITION — seeding the lexer and parser stages

Ordering is explicitly NOT a completion criterion. Any order satisfies this.

All of the following must hold as observable properties of the tree.

1. The lexer and parser self-hosted stages are each driven from real
   corpus input at more than one subject, or the tree records why a
   given one cannot be and names the obstacle.

2. Each seeded run is shown to be non-vacuous by a floor that would
   fail if the seed did not arrive. A non-empty result alone does not
   satisfy this.

3. The parser's subject is derived from the lexer's own output, and
   each subject is checked to produce enough tokens to drive the parser
   substantively rather than merely to run it.

4. The tree states plainly that these stages were already compared by
   another harness, so the gain is presented as additional subjects and
   gate accounting rather than as new coverage.

5. The report states how many stages are seeded and how many remain
   unseeded, and names the remaining unseeded stages rather than only
   counting them.

6. Files owned by the other line are unmodified. If seeding a stage
   would require changing them, that stage is reported as blocked
   instead.

7. A subject that does not fit the shared segment, or that fails to
   seed, is reported rather than silently skipped or truncated.

8. Any stage that disagrees on a newly added subject is reported as a
   finding and investigated, not exempted or masked.

9. The tree does not claim the Order-1 gate is met, and does not imply
   the unseeded set is closed.

10. Any claim that a figure changed is supported by a recorded
    measurement of both the before and after values.

11. If the subject count is bounded for runtime, the bound and its
    reason are stated rather than applied silently.

12. The native code generator's suite passes with zero failures and its
    formatting and lint checks pass, with the pass/fail judgement taken
    from something other than a truncated or piped view of the output.

13. Work that is finished is committed. Pushing may be deferred while a
    pull request of this line is mid-verification, provided the
    deferral is stated and the work is not left uncommitted.

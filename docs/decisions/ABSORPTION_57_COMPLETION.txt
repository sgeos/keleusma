COMPLETION CONDITION — absorption 57 and the width-discipline census

Ordering is NOT a completion criterion. Any order of the work satisfies this, and
nothing here requires a particular branch, commit shape, or process step.

All of the following hold in the tree:

1. THE ABSORPTION PREDICTION IS RECORDED BEFORE ITS OUTCOME.
   A document in the tree states, in advance of the merge, how many files were
   expected to conflict, which ones, and what was expected of the suite. The
   outcome is recorded beside it, stating for each clause whether it hit, and
   naming any clause that missed rather than quietly restating the prediction.

2. THE OWNERSHIP BOUNDARY IS VERIFIED, NOT ASSUMED.
   After absorbing, the repository-root src/ and tests/ are byte-identical to the
   other line's branch, checked rather than asserted.

3. THE NAMED RISK IS CLEARED BY EVIDENCE, NOT BY A GREEN AGGREGATE.
   The incoming set changes files that are also corpus subjects of this backend.
   Somewhere in the tree, the corpus-derived figures affected by that are
   re-derived and stated -- module count, refusal set, and any site or chunk
   figure pinned to an exact number -- rather than left to a passing suite.

4. EVERY PLACE THE EMITTER MOVES AN OPERAND AS A WORD IS ENUMERATED.
   A single place in the tree lists those sites and classifies each by whether a
   composite body operand can reach it, and if so what stops the address being
   stored where it would outlive the memory it points into. A site with no answer
   is stated as open rather than omitted.

5. THAT ENUMERATION IS NON-VACUOUS AND FAILS ON GROWTH.
   It finds the sites already known -- including the data-slot site that was a
   defect -- and a new site of the same kind causes a failure that asks for
   classification rather than passing silently.

6. THE CENSUS STATES WHAT IT CANNOT DO.
   It does not claim to prove any individual site is guarded, and says so next to
   itself.

7. NO NEW OPCODE, NO BYTECODE_VERSION CHANGE, AND NO EDIT TO src/ OR tests/ AT
   THE REPOSITORY ROOT OTHER THAN WHAT THE ABSORPTION BRINGS IN.

8. THE BACKEND SUITE IS GREEN IN BOTH FLOAT CONFIGURATIONS, with counts
   re-derived rather than quoted.

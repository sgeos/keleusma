COMPLETION CONDITION — the resume channels (V0.3.X)

Ordering is NOT a completion criterion. The properties below may be reached in
any order, by any route. Judge the end state of the tree and the transcript, not
the sequence, the branch, or the process used to get there.

DONE when all of the following hold.

1. THE APPEND-ONLY CHANNEL COVERS THE SESSION.
   The append-only journal contains an entry for this session's work, and that
   entry is not the newest-but-one behind increments that have since landed.

2. IT RECORDS REASONING, NOT A CHANGELOG.
   The entry says why things were done, including decisions taken against doing
   something. A list of what landed does not satisfy this on its own, because
   that list exists elsewhere.

3. REVERSALS APPEAR AS REVERSALS.
   Any position recorded during the session and later changed is present in both
   forms, with the superseded reasoning visible rather than replaced.

4. ERRORS ARE PRESENT, NOT ONLY OUTCOMES.
   Mistakes made and corrected during the session appear, including ones that
   were caught only by measurement rather than by review.

5. FIGURES ARE CARRIED, NOT RE-DERIVED.
   Every count in the entry is marked as carried from an existing measurement
   and states what it ranges over.

6. THE RESUME DOCUMENT MATCHES THE TREE.
   The handoff describes the branch as it now stands, including the absorptions
   completed and the open items with their owners.

7. ITS VALIDITY CHECK IS EXECUTED AND ITS RESULT REPORTED.
   The transcript shows the ancestry check running, with anchors covering the
   work since the previous stamp.

8. A STATED BLOCKER'S REASON CURRENTLY HOLDS.
   Any item described as blocked gives a reason that is true now. A reason that
   has expired is replaced, and the replacement does not imply the block has
   lifted if it has not.

9. THE VERIFICATION RESIDUAL IS STATED, NOT IMPLIED EITHER WAY.
   Where the branch has been verified under fewer configurations than the
   project supports, that is said explicitly, with the reason the remaining risk
   is judged low and an acknowledgement that it is not zero.

10. NOTHING IS CLAIMED THAT WAS NOT RUN.
    Every count and pass/fail claim corresponds to output appearing in the
    transcript.

11. THE PACKAGE IS GREEN, OR THE RED IS OWNED AND EXPLAINED.
    The native_codegen suite passes, or each failure is named with its cause and
    with a statement of whether the cause belongs to this package.

NOT completion criteria: which branch anything sits on, whether work is
committed, how many commits were made, the order of the eleven properties above,
or whether the session continues afterwards.

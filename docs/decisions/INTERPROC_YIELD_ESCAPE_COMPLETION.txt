3. INTERPROCEDURAL RESIDUAL IS BOUNDED BY MEASUREMENT
   An executable check in the tree reports, over the shipped corpus, how many
   chunks carry a composite construction site inside a loop whose value could
   reach a yield in another chunk. The figure is produced by running code, not
   asserted in prose. The check states plainly which direction it errs in, and
   distinguishes "a callee can yield" from "the composite reaches that yield"
   rather than treating them as the same fact. Any walk over the call graph
   terminates by construction on cyclic input.

4. THE MEASUREMENT GOVERNS THE DISPOSITION
   Either the backend refuses the interprocedural shape and the tree records a
   measured count of chunks newly refused as a result, or it does not refuse it
   and the tree records why, naming the cost that made refusal unacceptable.
   Either outcome is acceptable. What is not acceptable is a refusal whose cost
   was never measured, or a decision not to refuse that is unexplained.
